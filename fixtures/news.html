Harbour

Night ferry returns after a winter in dry dock

The old blue ferry is back on the eleven o'clock crossing, with new benches, a warmer cabin and the same slow horn.

By the harbour desk · 4 min read

Crews spent the cold months scraping the hull and refitting the engine room. The first sailing on Monday left the slipway twelve minutes late, which regulars said was about right.

Tickets are unchanged for now. The harbour office says a return fare review will come to the spring council meeting, along with a plan for a second bicycle rack on the lower deck.
