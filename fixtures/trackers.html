Millbrook Market Gazette

The Saturday market moves indoors from November, to the old corn hall on Bridge Street. Stalls open at eight.

The cheese stall has a new washed-rind round this month, and the baker has promised her sourdough will be back by Easter.

Notices

Parking on Bridge Street is suspended on market mornings. The car park behind the library stays open until noon.

Volunteers are wanted to help stack chairs after the winter fair. Ask at the information desk.
