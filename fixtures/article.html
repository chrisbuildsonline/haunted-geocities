How to Keep a Lighthouse Lamp Burning

By the keeper of North Point, ◷ 12 minutes to read

A lamp that goes out at two in the morning is a lamp nobody trimmed at dusk. The wick wants cutting square, the chimney wants a wipe, and the oil wants checking before the light is lit, not after.

The nightly round

Climb at sunset with a full can and a clean rag. Read the keeper's manual once a season; it is short, and it is right about the wick.

Trimming the wick

Cut straight across with the brass scissors. A ragged wick smokes, and smoke on the lens costs you a mile of light.

wick: trim square
chimney: wipe
oil: top up to the line

Logging the night

Write the weather, the hours lit and anything that passed. The log book is kept in the watch room.
